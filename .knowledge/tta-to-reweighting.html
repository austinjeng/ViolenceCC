The bug, the zero,
and the reroute.

How this paper's test-time adaptation story went from "TENT/SAR helps a little" to "entropy minimization provably does nothing — so we route between modalities instead."

Window 2026-06-04 → 06-11 Benchmark UCF-Crime-C (4 corruptions × 5 severities) Verdict +1.21pp mean corrupted-AUC, 3 seeds, 4/4 backbones

The original TENT/SAR "improvement" was a dropout bug, not adaptation. After making TTA deterministic and fixing a protocol that silently never adapted anything, the honest result is that entropy minimization moves corrupted-AUC by < 0.1 percentage points on every backbone — a structural limit of a frozen, rank-scored architecture. Feature-statistics restoration (CORAL-style) also failed: it reaches the right space but scrambles rankings. The replacement, discriminative-reliability reweighting, adapts zero parameters: it detects when the visual stream can no longer separate snippets and routes the gated fusion toward the corruption-robust skeleton stream. Validated over three seeds: +1.21pp mean, positive on all four backbones on every seed, up to +13.2pp where it matters most.

00Sixty seconds of context

The system detects violent anomalies in surveillance video. Two frozen encoders — a CTR-GCN over human skeletons (256-d) and a vision-language backbone over frames (1024–3072-d after pooling) — feed a small trainable gated fusion head plus MIL scorer (the only learned parts, 0.5–1.0M parameters). The question behind all TTA work: when deployment video is corrupted (sensor noise, blur, compression, lighting), can the model adapt at test time, with no labels?

The metric is a ranking

AUC only cares about the order of frame scores (anomalous above normal). Any adaptation that shifts scores without reordering them changes nothing. This single fact explains most of this story.

Almost everything is frozen

Backbones, fusion gate, and MIL head stay fixed at test time. Entropy-TTA may only touch the fusion head's three LayerNorms — 1,536 affine scalars (γ, β). The corruption damage lives upstream, in the frozen features.

Two streams, unequal damage

Pixel corruptions hit the visual-language stream hard. The skeleton stream is often untouched — for Gaussian noise and brightness the pipeline reuses the clean skeleton cache. Asymmetry is an asset.

Glossary — expand if any term is unfamiliar
TTA
Test-time adaptation: adjusting a model at inference, using only unlabeled test data.
TENT / SAR
Entropy-minimization TTA methods (ICLR '21 / '23). They sharpen prediction confidence by updating normalization-layer parameters per test batch.
LayerNorm (LN)
Per-sample normalization. Unlike BatchNorm it keeps no running dataset statistics — there is less for TTA to recalibrate.
CORAL / NORM
Aligning test-feature mean/covariance to clean-training statistics; a label-free "undo the shift" attempt.
UCF-Crime-C
The UCF-Crime test set under 4 synthetic corruptions (Gaussian noise, motion blur, JPEG, brightness) × 5 severities = 20 conditions.
Transductive
Statistics pooled over a whole corruption condition at once (batch access), rather than streaming frame-by-frame.
pp
Percentage points of AUC.

01Five stages, four reversals

The original claim collapses

Artifact

2026-06-04 · task 260604-w2a · commit 83b8927

The early draft reported a small TENT/SAR gain ("+0.6% CLIP SAR"). Audit found that configure_model set the LayerNorms to train mode but left the MIL head's nn.Dropout layers active at test time. TTA scores were stochastic — ~1.07pp per-condition AUC noise, 0.24pp on the 20-condition mean. The reported gain was dropout luck.

Fix: disable all dropout during TTA + seed everything. Re-run twice, get bit-identical AUC. The "+0.6%" evaporated.

The corrected zero was also fake

Protocol fix

2026-06-06 · task 260606-3hc · commit 1ed2d16

Deterministic re-runs gave exactly +0.000pp everywhere — too clean. Root cause: under the episodic per-video protocol, 98.4% of UCF test videos fit in a single chunk, so the adapted parameters never touched a scored output. The model was being graded on an exam it never sat.

We switched to TENT/SAR's native continual-online protocol: one reset per corruption stream, then adapt across the whole condition. A sanity probe confirmed adaptation now bites (+0.24pp on a single condition) — so the next zero would be a real zero.

The real negative result

Honest zero

validated 3-seed on 2026-06-09 · task 260610-46n · commit a5e3f73 · 477 runs

Under the valid protocol, TENT and SAR change mean corrupted-AUC by less than 0.1pp on every backbone (worst 3-seed |Δ| = 0.082pp). And the mechanism is structural, not a tuning issue: with backbone, gate, and head frozen, entropy descent can only nudge 1,536 LN affine scalars, while the corruption shift lives upstream in frozen features. Nudges that small cannot reorder snippet rankings — and AUC is purely a ranking.

This became a publishable claim instead of an embarrassment: entropy-TTA's failure here is a property of frozen LayerNorm heads + rank metrics, which nobody had pinned down for VAD.

The obvious alternative dead-ends

Dead end

2026-06-06 → 06-07 · CORAL/NORM de-risking · results/_coral_derisk/

If parameters can't be adapted, restore the features: align corrupted test statistics to clean-training statistics (NORM/CORAL-style). Exploratory covariance-CORAL looked promising (~+0.9pp on 3 of 4 backbones). Held-out validation killed it: per-channel whitening reorders the discriminative directions on the SigLIP2 backbones, the effect's sign flips across backbones, and leave-one-condition-out scored −1.62 with 0/4 backbones positive. No label-free operating point survives.

Diagnosis sharpened: the failure is not "wrong statistics," it is rank disruption. Whatever works must preserve the score ordering the model already produces.

The reroute: discriminative-reliability reweighting

Breakthrough

2026-06-07 · 6-strategy search → R1 · task 260607-42h · commit def88d5

Both failures pointed at the same opening: don't adapt parameters, don't move features — change which modality the frozen fusion trusts. A six-strategy parallel search (shift-gated, channel-selection, projection-space, fusion-gate, skeleton-blend, open-ended) produced R1: estimate visual-stream reliability from the spread of its own solo anomaly scores, and when that spread collapses — i.e., the stream can no longer tell snippets apart — scale the visual contribution down so the fusion leans on the corruption-robust skeleton.

Label-free, tuning-free, zero parameter updates. Production check: CLIP @ Gaussian severity 5 went 57.70 → 62.53 (+4.83), reproducing the search result exactly.

02How the mechanism works

Three scalars per corruption condition, all computed from unlabeled data:

wvl = min(1, σ(scliptest) / σ(sclipclean))   — visual reliability

σ(·) is the spread of the visual stream's solo anomaly scores (skeleton zeroed). Corruption that destroys the stream's ability to separate snippets collapses this spread. It is a discriminative signal — about score separation — not a distributional one. That distinction is precisely what CORAL got wrong.

s = min(1, max(0, (2z0 − zskel) / z0))   — skeleton-trust gate

zskel measures how shifted the skeleton features are vs. clean training; z0 is the natural clean-train→clean-test shift. If the skeleton is itself corrupted (motion blur, JPEG re-extract poses from degraded frames), s → 0 and no rerouting happens. Under our corruptions s saturates to {0, 1}, so the anchor "2" is never load-bearing.

w = 1 − (1 − wvl) · s   — applied to the projected visual features (after LN) in both gated and residual terms

Reroute toward the skeleton exactly when the visual stream collapses and the skeleton is still clean. If either condition fails, w → 1 and the model is bit-identical to source. The method can only act where it is safe to act.

Fig. A — No parameter is updated anywhere. The only test-time action is a per-condition scalar on the visual stream's projected features; when both streams are healthy, w = 1 and the model is exactly the source model.

The zeros are not the method failing — they are the gate working. For motion blur and JPEG, the skeleton is re-extracted from degraded frames, so s → 0 and the model stays at source (no harm done). For brightness, the visual stream remains reliable, so wvl ≈ 1. The gain is concentrated exactly where one modality dies and the other survives: Gaussian noise, peaking at +13.2pp (SO400M, severity 5, 3-seed mean).

What this does not claim

  • It is rescue, not robustness. The Gaussian gain comes from routing to a skeleton cache the corruption never touched — bounded by the skeleton-fusion ceiling, not evidence the fused features are intrinsically robust.
  • It is transductive. Statistics are pooled over a whole corruption condition (standard in this benchmark setting); a streaming/causal variant is future work.
  • The proxy can over-route. On one seed, SigLIP2-Base lost up to 3.2pp at high-severity Gaussian: its visual scores compressed without losing rank, and the spread proxy mistook compression for collapse. (That backbone is still positive on all three seeds on average.)
  • The entropy-TTA zero is empirical for this frozen-head, rank-metric configuration — stated as an established structural observation, not a universal theorem.
