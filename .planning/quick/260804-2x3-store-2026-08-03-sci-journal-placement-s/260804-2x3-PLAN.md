## Deviation from the standard quick workflow

Executed sequentially on the main tree by the orchestrator rather than delegated to
`gsd-planner` + `gsd-executor` in a worktree. Reason: the deliverable *is* the research
output, which lives entirely in the orchestrator's context. A fresh executor subagent
would have to reconstruct ~2.4M tokens of verified findings it has no access to, and
would produce a materially worse document. `workflow.use_worktrees` is unset in this
project's config, and the working tree carries substantial unrelated uncommitted changes,
so worktree isolation was also declined; the commit stages only the three files above.
