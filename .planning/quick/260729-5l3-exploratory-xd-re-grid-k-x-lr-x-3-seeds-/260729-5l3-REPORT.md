## Interpretation

1. **The historical gain does not transfer.** Both Appendix C confirmation winners invert sign: (1e-3, k2) +3.72 → −3.27 pp; (7e-4, k2) +3.39 → −5.39 pp. The gains were artifacts of the historical regime (CLIP ViT-B/16 features + pre-λ₂=0 smoothness), not properties of the task.
2. **The shipped configuration is locally optimal.** No cell in the re-run grid beats the headline cell; no single seed in any non-anchor cell beats the anchor's best seed (79.74). The uniform cross-dataset hyperparameter choice (lr 1e-4, k=3) is not leaving XD performance on the table — at least not within this ridge region.
3. **Appendix C's disposition conjecture is now empirically supported.** The appendix argued the sweep winner was deliberately not adopted partly because "the later, independently-adopted λ₂=0 setting recovered most of the XD-Violence headroom through a different mechanism." This re-grid directly shows the mechanism-overlap: once the final regime is in place, the ridge's headroom is not merely captured — the ridge direction reverses.
4. **Magnitude structure:** the only near-neutral deviation is k=2 at the default lr (−0.74, within ~1 seed-std — solid evidence of "no gain," weak evidence of harm). All higher-lr cells are decisively worse (−3 to −5.4 pp, multiple seed-stds). The final config sits in a sharper lr optimum than the historical one, where lr 1e-3 helped.
5. **Plausible mechanism (hypothesis, not conclusion):** the 2304-dim SO400M features give the fusion head a much richer input than 512-dim ViT-B/16, so lr 1e-4 no longer under-trains it (killing the high-lr leg of the ridge); with λ₂=0 the score profile can already spike sharply on short anomalies, removing the advantage of sharper top-k selection (killing the k≤2 leg).
