\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Weakly supervised video anomaly detection (VAD) localizes anomalous events
from video-level labels alone, yet most existing systems rely on a single
visual modality and assume fixed deployment conditions. This thesis presents
a dual-modal fusion framework that combines skeleton dynamics from a frozen
CTR-GCN encoder with features from frozen vision-language backbones, trained
with a multiple-instance-learning ranking loss; only the lightweight fusion
head is learned. The study spans four visual backbones --- CLIP ViT-B/16,
SigLIP2 ViT-B/16, SigLIP2 SO400M, and SigLIP2 Giant --- on the UCF-Crime and
XD-Violence benchmarks.

Gated fusion with learned modality weighting achieves 82.5\% frame-level AUC
on UCF-Crime (SigLIP2 Giant) and 78.7\% AP on XD-Violence (SigLIP2 SO400M),
each a three-seed mean. Skeleton features add a small but consistent
complementary gain: gated fusion meets or exceeds the visual-only baseline in
all eight backbone--dataset settings, strictly positive in six, despite the
skeleton's lower standalone performance. These numbers trail the field's
fine-tuned and text-aligned leaders, which reach roughly 88--91\% UCF-Crime
AUC --- a gap that follows from the deliberately frozen, no-text design. The
contribution is a reproducible, single-GPU dual-modal pipeline and a
label-free robustness study, not a new peak score.

A second investigation studies test-time adaptation (TTA) under 20 synthetic
corruption conditions over three seeds. Entropy-minimization TTA (TENT, SAR),
restricted by the architecture to the fusion head's LayerNorm parameters,
changes AUC by less than $0.1$ points on every backbone --- a structural null
result analyzed in the thesis body. What does recover robustness is a
proposed label-free, tuning-free discriminative-reliability reweighting that
routes the gated fusion toward the corruption-robust skeleton stream when the
visual-language stream's discriminative signal collapses: corrupted-input AUC
improves on all four backbones (mean $+1.2$ points, up to $+13.2$ points in
the single most-degraded condition), under a transductive protocol and with
no gain claimed on clean data.
