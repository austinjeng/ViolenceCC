\section*{Symbols}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{10.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning \\
\midrule

$\mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_v}$ & visual snippet feature (pooled VLM embedding) \\

$\hat{\mathbf{s}}$, $\hat{\mathbf{v}}$ & projected, LayerNorm-normalized

$W_s, W_v, W_g$; $\mathbf{b}_s, \mathbf{b}_v, \mathbf{b}_g$ & learned
             projection and gate weights / biases \\
$\mathbf{g} \in [0,1]^{256}$ & per-dimension gating vector \\
$\odot$ & element-wise (Hadamard) product \\
$\mathbf{f}_{\text{gated}}$ & fused snippet representation \\
$\text{LN}_s$, $\text{LN}_v$, $\text{LN}_f$ & the three LayerNorm modules of
             the fusion head (skeleton projection, visual projection, fused
             output) \\
$\gamma$, $\beta$ & LayerNorm affine scale and shift (the TENT/SAR adaptation
             surface) \\
$a$, $a_i$, $a_{\text{late}}$ & snippet anomaly score; late-fusion score \\
$\mathbf{a}_t$ & abnormal-bag anomaly-score vector at snippet position $t$
             (sparsity term of Eq.~\ref{eq:loss}) \\
$T$ & fixed training bag length (32 snippets) \\

             joints ($V=17$), channels ($C=3$) \\

             weights \\
$p_{\mathrm{clip}}$ & LayerNorm-projected visual-language features (the
             reweighting target) \\
$s_{\mathrm{clip}}$ & solo visual snippet scores (fusion head run with the
             skeleton stream zeroed) \\
$\sigma(\cdot)$ & logistic sigmoid (gate computation,
             Chapter~\ref{ch:03}); standard deviation pooled over a
             corruption condition (reliability estimate,
             Chapters~\ref{ch:03} and~\ref{ch:06}) --- context disambiguates \\
$w_{\mathrm{vl}}$ & visual-reliability score (solo-score dispersion ratio,
             clipped to $[0,1]$) \\
$s$ (scalar) & skeleton-reliability gate in $[0,1]$ \\

\medskip
\noindent\textit{Remark.} Bold lowercase symbols denote vectors; plain
lowercase symbols denote scalars. The scalar $s$ (skeleton-reliability gate)
and the vector $\mathbf{s}$ (skeleton feature) are distinct quantities, as
are the two context-dependent uses of $\sigma(\cdot)$ noted above; both
conventions follow the notation paragraph of Chapter~\ref{ch:03}.
